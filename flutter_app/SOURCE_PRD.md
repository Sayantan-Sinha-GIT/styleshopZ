# Flutter app product requirements

The current, authoritative product requirements are in the [styleshopZ PRD](../PRD.md). The [Flutter README](README.md) describes the native app's build steps and supported shopper and seller flows.

The Flutter APK is for shoppers and sellers. Owner administration remains on the website and in the separate owner-only Capacitor APK. Checkout, courier events, refunds and seller payouts remain test-mode or simulated prototype flows, as described in the main PRD and [release QA matrix](../QA_MATRIX.md).
